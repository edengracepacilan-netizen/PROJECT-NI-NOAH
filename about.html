Who We Are

Salamence Tracker is a logistics and supply chain management system designed to help organize warehouse operations, inventory stocks, vehicle tracking, and supplier orders in one platform.

🎯

Our Mission

To make logistics operations easier by helping users manage inventory, monitor deliveries, and organize supply chain activities efficiently.

🔭

Our Vision

To provide an organized and reliable platform for managing logistics information and supporting better supply chain decisions.

Our System Features

Feature Description
Dashboard Overview of logistics activities.
Warehouse Organization of warehouse information.
Inventory Stocks Management of products and stock levels.
Fleet Tracking Monitoring of vehicles and deliveries.
Supplier Orders Organization of supplier orders.
AI Assistant Assistance with supply chain tasks.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make supply chain information easier to access, understand, and manage.
